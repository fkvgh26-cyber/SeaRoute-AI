IMPORTANT LIMITATION

This prototype is not a navigation authority.

It does not connect to live marine weather, tides, GPS, charts, vessel tracking, or emergency services. Do not use it to choose a route or decide whether to depart. Check official Bangladesh weather and maritime warnings, follow local authorities, and use qualified human judgment.

WHY SEAROUTE AI

Designed around people, not just technology.

SeaRoute AI is an early-stage concept for coastal fishing communities. The goal is to make trip preparation and safety information easier to organize, including for users who prefer Bangla.

Responsible use means being honest about uncertainty, protecting personal information, testing with local fishers, and keeping safety-critical decisions with people and official sources.
